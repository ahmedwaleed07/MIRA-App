Delete your MIRA account

You can request deletion from MIRA inside the app through Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, submit a support request and include the phone number or email used for the account so ownership can be verified before deletion.

Deletion removes the account and associated profile data, favorites, follows, preferences, and user-generated support data, except information that must be retained for fraud prevention, security, legal, or accounting obligations.
