MIRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

MIRA is an offers discovery platform. The app may process account information, approximate or precise location when the user grants permission, selected interests, saved offers, followed stores, notification preferences, support messages, and merchant business information.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, personalize offers, show nearby offers, operate merchant tools, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, and provide support.

Location

Location is optional. Users can deny or revoke location access in device settings and continue using manual market and city selection.

Sharing

MIRA does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate authentication, hosting, notifications, analytics, maps, and support, subject to their service terms and applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Users may request account deletion from the app or the account deletion page. Data that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal, or accounting obligations may be retained only as required.

Children

MIRA is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

Contact

Privacy requests can be submitted through the in-app Help & Support section.
