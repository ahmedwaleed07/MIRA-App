# MIRA Store Listing

## App name
MIRA

## Subtitle / short description
Offers all year round

## Full description
MIRA is a location-aware shopping offers platform that helps people discover nearby and nationwide promotions from verified merchants. Users can browse flash sales, explore categories, search offers and stores, save favorites, follow merchants, receive offer notifications, and manage shopping preferences by market and city.

For merchants, MIRA provides tools to publish offers, manage branches, schedule promotions, view analytics, and manage subscriptions. Administrative tools support merchant verification, offer moderation, campaign management, markets, reporting, and platform analytics.

MIRA focuses on offer discovery. Unless a future commerce feature is explicitly enabled, products and fulfillment remain the responsibility of the merchant.
